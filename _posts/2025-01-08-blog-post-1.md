I have two cats, one is a British Shorthair, and the other is also British Shorthair. They are so beautiful,cute,docile and always stay with me when I sleep or read,or do other things. I love them very much.

They can take care of themseves,but they need special attention. Owning a cat is almost like begin a parent. I think about what they are need and provide food,water,a safe home and toys like cat teasers to play with them.

Usually, the cats curl up on the sofa or on foot of the bed, when they feel content, they purr. for example, they purr when I give them cat treats or play with them. Sometimes they run around the room in bursts of energy, a behaviour known as the "zoomies".

In the past, my wife was afraid to go near cats. however, since we got a cat, she is not longer afraid to approach or hold them. Now she's not afraid of cats anymore!
