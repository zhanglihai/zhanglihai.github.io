## About This Blog

I first started building websites back in 2000, on platforms like 263.net and 163.com — that was when I caught the bug for writing online. Later, I ran a few blogs on [WordPress](https://wordpress.com/), but they all vanished into the void because I forgot to renew the hosting. A classic tale.

In January 2025, the urge to write came back. I wanted a place to put down thoughts on life, books, technology, and whatever else crosses my mind. This time, I went with [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com) — no hosting fees to forget, no databases to maintain. Just code and words.

## What You'll Find Here

- Reflections on the **AI era** and what it means for those of us who write code for a living
- Nostalgia for the **early days of computing** — dial-up modems, Windows 95, and the people who made magic with limited tools
- Notes on **books, life, and the occasional philosophical tangent**

## About Me

I'm a software engineer based in Beijing, working at Alibaba Cloud on security products. I've been writing code for over two decades — mostly Java, Python, and Go. Outside of work, I enjoy reading, thinking about where technology is heading, and spending time with my two British Shorthair cats.
